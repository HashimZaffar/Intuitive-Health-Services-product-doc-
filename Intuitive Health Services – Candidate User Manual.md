# **1. Introduction**

Welcome to the **Intuitive Health Services Locum Staffing Platform**, a digital portal designed to help healthcare professionals discover, apply for, and manage locum tenens opportunities across **California, New York**, and additional states as expansion continues.

This guide helps candidates:

* Create an account
* Build a complete professional profile
* Upload credentials and required documents
* Browse and apply for locum roles
* Track application status
* Communicate with recruiters
* Manage assignments

This manual is written for:
**Registered Nurses, Physicians, Psychiatrists, Social Workers, Therapists, CNAs, LVNs, and all allied health professionals** supported by Intuitive Health Services.

---

# **2. Getting Started**

## **2.1 System Requirements**

The platform works on:

* Any modern browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
* Laptop, desktop, tablet, or mobile (responsive web)
* Stable internet connection

No installation needed.

---

## **2.2 Create an Account**

### Steps:

1. Visit the Intuitive Health Services candidate portal.
2. Click **“Sign Up”**.
3. Enter:

   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Email
   * Password
4. Select your **discipline** (e.g., RN, Psychiatrist).
5. Accept Terms & Conditions.
6. Click **Create Account**.

### Verification:

A confirmation email is sent instantly. Click the verification link to activate your account.

---

# **3. Candidate Dashboard Overview**

After logging in, you’ll land on your **main dashboard**, containing:

* **Profile Completion Status**
* **Recommended Jobs**
* **Recently Viewed Roles**
* **Uploaded Credentials**
* **Messages from Recruiters**
* **Current Assignment Status**

Each section is interactive and guides you through next steps.

---

# **4. Building Your Profile**

A complete profile improves your job-match score and helps recruiters find roles faster.

## **4.1 Required Profile Fields**

* Full Name
* Phone Number
* Primary Discipline
* Years of Experience
* Preferred Locations (e.g., Fresno, Los Angeles)
* Availability (Full-time, Part-time, Contract)

## **4.2 Professional Summary**

Provide a short description of your experience and career goals.

### Example:

“Registered Nurse with 5+ years in acute care and correctional facility settings. Seeking flexible locum assignments in Central California.”

---

## **4.3 Uploading Your Resume**

1. Go to **Profile → Resume**
2. Upload a PDF or DOCX file
3. The system will run an **optional resume assessment** to evaluate job-fit strength
4. Results are shown in your dashboard

Supported File Types: `.pdf`, `.docx`
Maximum Size: 10 MB

---

# **5. Managing Credentials & Licenses**

Intuitive Health Services requires up-to-date credentials before you can be placed in an assignment.

## **5.1 Upload Credential Documents**

Common uploads include:

* State Licenses (RN, MD, LCSW, etc.)
* Certifications (BLS, ACLS)
* Background Check Reports
* Vaccination Records
* Compliance Documents

### Steps:

1. Go to **Credentials**
2. Click **Upload New Document**
3. Select a category
4. Upload PDF/JPEG
5. Click **Submit**

The status will appear as:

* **Pending Review**
* **Verified**
* **Needs Resubmission**

Recruiters or credentialing specialists will update statuses.

---

# **6. Browsing Jobs**

The job marketplace shows all open roles across participating facilities (e.g., correctional facilities and state hospitals in California).

## **6.1 Using Filters**

You can filter jobs by:

* Discipline (RN, MD, CNA, etc.)
* Location
* Pay Type (Daily, Hourly)
* Facility Type (Hospital, Correctional, etc.)
* Shift Type
* Experience Required

---

## **6.2 Viewing Job Details**

Each listing includes:

* Position title
* Facility location
* Job description
* Pay range (if available)
* Shift and schedule
* Requirements
* Recruiter contact
* Posted date

Click **“View Details”** to see complete information.

---

# **7. Applying for Jobs**

## **7.1 How to Apply**

1. Open a job listing
2. Click **“Apply Now”**
3. Confirm your resume & profile
4. Submit

You will receive a confirmation notification.

---

## **7.2 Tracking Your Applications**

Navigate to **Applications** to view status:

| Status              | Meaning                          |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Submitted           | Application received             |
| Under Review        | Recruiter reviewing your profile |
| Interview Scheduled | Recruiter requests meeting       |
| Credentialing       | License verification in progress |
| Offer Extended      | Assignment available             |
| Accepted            | You accepted the role            |
| Rejected            | Position filled or mismatch      |

---

* Application confirmations

# **9. Managing Assignments**

Once hired for a locum position, your assignment card shows:

* Start date / End date
* Facility name
* Shift schedule
* Reporting instructions
* Required specialty documents
* Recruiter contact

## **9.1 Assignment Statuses**

* **Upcoming**
* **Active**
* **Completed**
* **Cancelled** (by facility or candidate)

# **10. Profile & Notification Settings**

## **10.1 Notification Preferences**

Enable/disable:

* Job recommendations
* Application updates
* Credential reminders
* Assignment updates

## **10.2 Update Password or Email**

Navigate to **Settings → Account**.

All sensitive actions require re-entering your password.

# **11. Troubleshooting & Support**

## **11.1 Common Issues**

| Issue                    | Solution                          |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Cannot upload credential | Check file size or format         |
| Not receiving emails     | Check spam folder; verify email   |
| Job list empty           | Adjust filters or expand location |
| Cannot apply             | Ensure profile is 80% complete    |
